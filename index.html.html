🏊 Swim — Gialova Bay
Sea temp: 23–24°C — no wetsuit required but legal to wear. Most athletes will opt for one.
Conditions: Ionian Sea is typically calm in October. Gialova Bay is sheltered — minimal chop expected.
Visibility: Crystal clear water. Sighting buoys will be easy to spot.
Start: Early morning means cooler air (~18°C) — get in the water to warm up.
🚴 Bike — Messinia roads
Course: Flat, fast, sweeping roads. Minimal climbing — ideal for aero position.
Temp on bike: 20–23°C by mid-morning. Comfortable but not cold — arm warmers not needed.
Wind: Light coastal breeze, 13 km/h average. May get slightly stronger in open sections — stay aero.
Sun: Ride heads roughly east-west — sun will be in your face on parts. Keep sunglasses on.
🏃 Run — Pine forest
Temp on run: 22–24°C — warm enough to feel the heat if you go out too fast.
Shade: Natura 2000 pine forest provides shade — the best possible run environment for late October heat.
Humidity: ~69% — Mediterranean humidity. Sweat won't evaporate as fast. Drink at every aid station.
Strategy: First 5km will feel hot after the bike. Start conservative. Pine forest is cooler.
Kit recommendations for these conditions
✓ Wetsuit: Legal (sea <24.5°C) and beneficial. Buoyancy advantage in 23°C water outweighs any warmth cost.
✓ Tri suit: Short-sleeve or sleeveless. No need for arm warmers — temps warm up quickly after 8am.
✓ Sunscreen: Essential. Mediterranean October sun is deceptively strong. Apply before swim start.
✓ Cap/visor on run: Recommended. Sun will be out and the run is exposed before the forest section.
✓ Hydration: Drink more than you think. 69% humidity means sweat doesn't evaporate — you dehydrate faster.
⚠ Rain risk: 15% chance. Pack a lightweight emergency layer in T1 bag — but don't overthink it. Odds are heavily in your favour.
Pace adjustment for heat: At 23°C+ with 69% humidity, expect your run pace to be 15–30 seconds per km slower than your training pace in cooler conditions. This is normal and not a failure — it is physics. Plan your run leg around effort rather than pace. Your training paces are calibrated for UK conditions — factor in the heat from km 1.
Source: 10+ years historic climate data, Methoni/Messinia meteorological stations · Costa Navarino, Peloponnese
