Calorie target

Aim for a 500–600 kcal daily deficit. At 108kg with high training volume, maintenance is around 2,800–3,000 kcal. Target 2,200–2,400 kcal on weekdays, 2,600–2,800 on big training days.

Protein priority

Hit 180–200g protein daily to preserve muscle while losing fat. Never skip protein on rest days — that is when muscle is built and repaired.

June–July sprint

Target 1.5–2kg loss per week in June and July. After July, aim for 0.5–1kg per week as training volume increases and your body needs more fuel.

Race weight note

Stop active dieting by mid-September. In the final 5 weeks focus entirely on fuelling training. Arriving slightly above 95kg is fine — arriving underfuelled is not.

Hosting on GitHub Pages (free)

Go to github.com and create a free account. Click New repository, name it ironman-training, set to Public. Upload both files — rename tri-portal.html to index.html (your homepage) and keep this file as ironman-703-training-plan.html. Then Settings → Pages → source: main branch → Save. Your site is live at https://yourusername.github.io/ironman-training within minutes. Share that link with your squad.

2

Hosting on Netlify (even easier)

Go to netlify.com and sign up free. Put both files in a folder — rename tri-portal.html to index.html. Drag the whole folder into the Netlify dashboard. You get a live link instantly. Rename it to something like myles-tri-squad.netlify.app for free in settings.

3

Your two files explained

index.html (the squad portal) — the login page everyone lands on. Squad members register and log in here, and can reach this full plan via the nav bar inside their dashboard.

ironman-703-training-plan.html — your detailed plan with Garmin analysis, swim drill videos, and structured sessions. Both files must live in the same folder for the links between them to work.

4

Updating your plan

Come back to Claude and say "update week X" or "change Thursday's session". You will get updated code to paste in. Open the file in Notepad (right-click → Open With → Notepad), make the change, save, then re-upload to GitHub or Netlify. Changes go live in seconds.

5

Logging 5K test results

After each time trial (weeks 8, 13, 16) go to the Paces tab and log your result. All zones update instantly. Jot results down in the weight log too for a permanent record.

6

FTP tests — what to do

On FTP test weeks (4 and 12), Wednesday bike intervals are replaced with: 10 min easy warm-up → 20 min all-out effort → 10 min cool-down. FTP = 95% of average power over the 20 min. Ask Claude to calculate your bike training zones from the wattage.
